Bioinformatics

Clinical genomics

Invent Technologies, Jun – Jul 2026

Germline variant calling and an automated reporting engine

As a bioinformatics intern, I ran germline variant calling on Illumina MiSeq data from the TruSight One panel (4,813 genes, GRCh38) with BWA-MEM, Samtools, Picard and GATK HaplotypeCaller, following GATK Best Practices. Reports were being formatted by hand, so I wrote a Python command-line tool that reads the pipeline’s metrics, coverage and VCF files and produces finished Word and PDF reports with CSV summaries.

My part
Variant-calling runs; design and build of the reporting engine; on-site requirement mapping at a client lab in Bogura
Built with
GATK, BWA-MEM, Samtools, Picard, Python, docxtpl, Jinja2, headless LibreOffice
Result
Report formatting no longer needs manual work

Machine learning

Personal project

r_u_promoter

Paste a DNA sequence and find out whether it’s likely a promoter. An XGBoost classifier trained on 60,000 labelled gene sequences (98% accuracy) runs behind an interactive app, and predictions are stored in MongoDB Atlas.

Built with
Python, XGBoost, Hugging Face Spaces, MongoDB Atlas

Project Korikath

An independent community of photographers and videographers documenting under-represented places and culture for Wikimedia Commons. I set its multi-year strategy, trust and safety guidance and partnerships, and managed the grant that bought its first camera. The project has kept growing since my term ended; its totals as of October 2026:

  • 96,000+ files from 640 contributors
  • 7,900+ pages on 193 wikis use them
  • 33 Commons Pictures of the Day

Founder and trainer

Notre Dame College, 2020–present

NDEC Wikipedia Editorial and Research Team

A student team I built from scratch to teach high schoolers open knowledge work. It began with taking our college’s Bangla Wikipedia article to Featured Article in four months, managing the conflict of interest in the open. Since then I have run its training: one bootcamp enrolled 128 students and produced 300 new Bangla Wikipedia articles, and a 2024 workshop reached 150–200 students. Alumni founded the Dhaka University Wikipedia Editorial and Research Team in 2026, which I advise and mentor.

About

I study Bioinformatics Engineering at Bangladesh Agricultural University. My technical work sits where computational biology, pipeline engineering and machine learning meet: I like software that other researchers can trust and run again.

The other half of my time has gone to open knowledge. Since 2020 I’ve built editing and photography teams, trained teachers and students, managed grants, and helped write policy for a global movement. Both halves come down to the same thing: making information usable by more people.
