About

I study Bioinformatics Engineering at Bangladesh Agricultural University, in its first cohort. My work sits where computational biology, pipeline engineering and machine learning meet: I like software that other researchers can trust and run again.

The other half of my time goes to open knowledge. Since 2020 I’ve built Wikipedia editing teams, trained teachers, drafted community policy and translated Wikimedia documents into Bangla. Both halves come down to the same thing: making information usable by more people.
